Three stages, about 200 hours in total (PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10). One playthrough is enough: there is no mission replay and no New Game+, but free roam continues after the story and nothing on the 100% checklist is missable. Save manually at the start of every chapter.

How the stages were built

Stage order follows the official descriptions: story trophies first (chapters and missions in order), then the 100% completion checklist for the Gold A Long Road, then the stranger strands and cleanup. Counts (200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 70 of 80 challenges, 39 gang operations, 22 bank jobs, 7 payday loan jobs, 8 bank vault robberies) come from PowerPyx and GTABase. Sources for each stage are listed under the stage.

Only one trophy is possibly missable. Everything else can be earned after the story in free roam.

Why the rest are safe

  • No mission replay, no New Game+. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission — Output Lag and PowerPyx both recommend this.
  • Cheats disable trophies and saving for that session, so keep them off your trophy run (GTABase).
  • No online trophies. All 55 are single-player; GTA VI launches as a single-player game (PowerPyx, GTABase).
  • 100% completion is not everything. The checklist asks for part of each category (for example 15 of 18 meditation spots), so a missed collectible rarely blocks the Gold (PowerPyx).
